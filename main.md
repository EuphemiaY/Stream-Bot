# Instructions
Make a keys.cjs with your BOT_USER_ID, OAUTH_TOKEN, CLIENT_ID, and CHAT_CHANNEL_USER_ID as constants if you want the chat to work
Update the ServerPort.json with the port your pokelink is running on

Probably also update your usernames in partyEuph.cjs to be yours and partyIvy.cjs to be your other pokelink player, or just log in with the same user names in the session as are in line 15 and 14 for those files.

You can enter "npm run UpdatePartiesRunBot" in your terminal and good things will happen if all is well.
